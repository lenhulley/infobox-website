Our story

Where we came from

Infobox grew out of two decades of consulting for organisations that needed technology to solve real operational problems — not vanity projects or digital transformations for their own sake.

We started as individual consultants working in the Microsoft ecosystem, building solutions for membership bodies and visitor attractions. Over time, we found that the same patterns kept emerging: organisations with great domain expertise but fragmented, outdated digital platforms holding them back.

Infobox was formalised as a consultancy to bring together the skills needed to solve these problems properly: CMS, CRM, mobile, cloud infrastructure, and — increasingly — responsible AI.
